Common questions

How do I add my ex, partner or a grandparent?

Go to More → Family → Invite someone new. Choose which of your children they can see and whether they are a guardian, co-parent or carer. Send them the code; they enter it after creating their own account.

Can the other parent see my children from another relationship?

No. Each adult only sees the children shared with them. Tap an adult on the Family screen to see or change exactly what they can see.

How do I set up our custody rota?

Open Calendar → Schedule, pick a pattern (or build your own two-week rota with handover times), and choose the start week.

Why am I not getting notifications?

Check Settings → Notifications → Kinloop on your phone, then the bell icon in Kinloop → Settings to see which alerts are switched on.

How do I delete my account?

Email us from the address you signed up with and we will delete your account and personal information within 30 days. See the privacy policy.
