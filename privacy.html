Kinloop

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Kinloop helps separated, blended and busy families organise children across more than one home. Because it holds information about children, we keep what we collect to the minimum the app needs, never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never track you across other apps or websites.

Who we are

Kinloop is made by Gavin Davey in the United Kingdom (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for information held in Kinloop. Contact: gavindavey1980@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: name and email address, used to sign you in.
  • Family information you add: children’s names, birthdays, school, year group and colour; households; custody schedules; calendar events and clubs; chores, routines, homework, points and rewards; shopping lists and meal plans.
  • Health information you choose to add: children’s allergies, medicines, dose times and whether doses were given.
  • Messages between adults in your family hub.
  • Photos and documents you choose to upload (for example a school letter). Kinloop only accesses the photos you pick.
  • Expenses you log between adults (amounts and descriptions only – Kinloop never takes payments or card details).
  • A push notification token for your device, so we can send the alerts you have switched on.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or analytics about how you use the app.

Who can see it

Information about a child is only visible to adults that child’s guardian has chosen to share with, at the level they chose (guardian, co-parent or carer). This is enforced on our servers, not just hidden in the app. Private events are visible only to the person who created them.

We use these service providers to run Kinloop:

  • Supabase – database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Expo – delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Apple – app distribution and push notification delivery.

We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, except where required by law.

Why we use it (legal basis)

We use your information to provide the service you signed up for (contract). Health information about children is processed only because you choose to add it, with your explicit consent, so you and the adults you share with can manage medicines and allergies. You can remove it at any time.

Children

Kinloop accounts are for adults. Children can use “kid mode” on a parent’s device, which shows a simplified view protected by a parent PIN; children do not create accounts or give us information directly.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete something in the app it is removed. If you ask us to delete your account we delete your personal information within 30 days, except where other adults in your family hub still rely on shared records they are entitled to keep.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your information, or object to how we use it. Email gavindavey1980@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Access is restricted per child by database-level security rules.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and tell you in the app for significant changes.
