Delete your Kinloop account

Kinloop: Co-Parenting Calendar is published by PaperPixel.media. You can delete your account and personal data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Kinloop and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Family & settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation and tap Delete account again.

Your account is deleted straight away. This cannot be undone.

Without the app

Email gavindavey1980@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, asking us to delete your account. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name and sign-in details.
  • Your notifications, notification settings and the push notification token for your devices.
  • If you are the only adult in your family: the whole family, including children's details, calendar, custody rota, chores, homework, rewards, medicines, expenses, messages and uploaded documents.

What is kept

If other adults are still in your family, things you added that they share with you stay so their records remain complete: calendar events, messages in the co-parent log, expenses and similar entries. They are no longer linked to you and show as from "a former member".

We keep no other copy of your data once it is deleted. Copies may remain in our database provider's routine backups for a short time until those backups expire.
